# New textbook chapters: research and artwork record

Research date: 2026-10-05. New original lessons cover AC/DC coupling, storage certification evidence and flexible loads/EV charging. Calculations use explicitly hypothetical values; they do not claim product performance or local installation approval.

Primary sources checked:

- Victron AC-coupling and Factor 1.0 rule: https://www.victronenergy.com/live/ac_coupling:start — topology-specific rule, not a generic industry limit.
- Victron ESS system design: https://www.victronenergy.com/media/pg/Energy_Storage_System/en/ess-system-design.html — supported architecture and battery acceptance boundaries.
- UL Solutions ESS certification: https://www.ul.com/services/energy-storage-system-testing-and-certification — component versus system evaluation.
- UL Solutions UL 9540A: https://www.ul.com/services/ul-9540a-test-method — fire-propagation test method, not interchangeable with a system listing.
- UL 9540 scope: https://www.shopulstandards.com/ProductDetail.aspx?ShowFreeviewModal=1&UniqueKey=44534 — matched assemblies and stated scope/revision.
- Emporia excess solar management: https://www.emporiaenergy.com/home-energy-management/excess-solar/ — supported controller example.
- Emporia charger installation guide: https://cdn.emporiaenergy.com/products/c2/Emporia-Pro-Charger-Install-Guide-English-091125.pdf — manufacturer configuration evidence.
- DOE smart charging: https://www.energy.gov/cmei/femp/smart-charge-management-applications-and-benefits-federal-fleets — fleet context, not household installation rules.

Figures are original SVG illustrations reproducible with `site/scripts/create-guide-diagrams.py`. They depict conceptual boundaries, module construction, IV relationships, geometry and evidence flow; they omit complete installation details deliberately. No third-party technical figure was copied. Where catalog photographs appear, their manufacturer/retailer source is linked and models are explicitly distinguished from calculated examples. Existing Solar4U provenance is retained on imported chapters.
