# Installation, operation and news chapter research ledger

Reviewed: 2026-10-05. Scope: the 16 replacement chapters in `site/data/guide-textbook-installation.json`. This is a source and editorial audit record, not a site engineering, tax, inspection or product-test approval.

## Editorial method and evidence boundaries

Primary manufacturer manuals, US government safety and permitting resources, national-laboratory publications, IRS instructions, Chinese tax-authority text and IEA analysis were checked through web retrieval. Chapters explain the principles in original prose. Worked examples use explicitly hypothetical inputs and show their assumptions and arithmetic; they are not observed offers, manufacturer performance results, structural capacities or electrical installation recipes. Tables organize original teaching comparisons rather than reproducing manufacturer tables.

Installation articles distinguish geometry, structural demand/capacity, roofing interfaces, electrical compatibility and local approval. They prescribe no universal attachment spacing, ballast mass, torque, setback or conductor size. The locally adopted code edition and exact equipment manual remain project-specific. OSHA material is explicitly identified as US guidance. Commissioning and troubleshooting reserve intrusive tests, isolation, energization and service for qualified personnel using the approved equipment-specific procedure. No switch-by-switch universal startup sequence is supplied.

A source being checked on October 5 means its content or official announcement was verified that day; it does not mean a future delivery, shipment target, marketing performance or individual project eligibility was independently proven. Company claims and forecasts remain attributed. Economic implications are identified as editorial analysis or hypothetical arithmetic.

## Original figures and numerical examples

Every article references an existing PVPartPicker original SVG ID. Captions were reviewed against the SVG title, description and labels. The low-slope chapter explicitly identifies `roof-load-path` as a pitched-roof illustration. Canopies use `inverter-topology`, troubleshooting uses `meter-boundaries`, and sodium storage identifies `battery-bank` as two hypothetical 51.2V batteries rather than a CATL diagram. Other IDs used are `ground-spacing`, `commissioning`, `cash-flow` and `module-anatomy`. None is an engineering-approved installation drawing.

Examples checked: roof mass and average load; row rise, footprint and pitch; roof-width uncertainty; complete mounting cost; local ballast pressure; lever-arm moment; canopy daily energy versus simultaneous charger power; commissioning interval-energy difference; AC energy reconciliation; cleaning cost-benefit; delivered quotes; battery usable energy; module packing; backup runtime; simple payback with an unsupported incentive removed; hypothetical replacement-service premium. All givens are marked as hypothetical; no example is an observed offer or an actual product test.
