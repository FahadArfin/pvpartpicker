## PVPartPicker implementation

The source registry now has 39 retailers: the 36 listed by drop.solar plus Emporia, WindyNation and TEMCo. Existing saved owner settings are preserved by insert-only seeding. Thirty sources have enabled defaults: nine existing sources and 21 newly verified USD public feeds. Nine new sources are initially paused, with review notes: Portable Sun, Anker, Orient Power, RUiXU, EcoFlow, SOK, Callsun, DJI and Docan. Their sitemap or origin alone does not prove a correctly priced USD product. An HTTP success is not a successful collection run.

New feed sources start with daily schedules and at least ten seconds between requests. Shopify variants remain separate listings; WooCommerce variable parents remain excluded. Feed pages rotate between runs, resetting on a short/empty final page. The job cursor only advances after a successfully parsed page. The existing 12-minute job budget, robots checks, per-domain spacing, cancellation, public-IP validation and no-challenge-bypass behavior remain in place. The next scheduled run resumes work rather than repeatedly inspecting page one.

Full-catalog refresh time depends on feed pages per run, product count and request budget. Product-page sources remain slower than bulk feeds. Site-owner controls are at `/price-scraper`; no scraper actions are exposed through WebMCP.

## SignatureSolar.7z

The forum archive was shared by **warklantd**, not generated by Smith: [post 1651990](https://diysolarforum.com/threads/price-history-tool-for-signature-solar-current-connected-shopsolar-rich-solar.117159/post-1651990). Smith's reply says he will investigate incorporation; it does not establish that drop.solar imported it.

Archive SHA-256: `fdace8ab0143588f1091f530126e7b2b7a2ee8b2a6aacbf6c9732b93d62b415c`.

The aggregate CSV contains 274,717 observations, 1,207 distinct product URLs and 500 dates from **2024-06-03 to 2025-11-28**. The 500 daily snapshot CSVs overlap the aggregate; the 500 price-analysis CSVs contain derived summaries. Import only the aggregate, deduplicated by offer/date. Do not count the other files as extra observations.

The initial current-catalog comparison produced **8,911 prices for 24 listings**. Unchanged titles and exact Signature Solar URLs qualify; a small explicit whitelist covers reviewed EG4 inverter aliases and the Peimar 31-panel pallet. Ambiguous bundles, changed connector options and unmatched/discontinued URLs stay in an unresolved report. Do not create current offers from old listings or fuzzy-match old prices to different products.

Source rows have a date but no time, stock, currency column or stable variant SKU. The import preserves day precision, marks stock unknown, attaches archive attribution and uses the reviewed listing's package quantity. Midnight UTC is a storage key, not an asserted collection time. The historical import writes only observations and provenance; it cannot update current prices, freshness, availability, product specs or alerts. Replaying a batch is idempotent. Historical gaps remain visible, and connecting chart points does not imply continuous collection.

## Reproduce and review

From the repository root, after extracting the supplied archive into an ignored output folder:

```powershell
node --experimental-strip-types site/scripts/import-history.mjs --plan output/signature-history-plan.json --csv output/signature-history-source/all_products_data.csv --catalog output/live-catalog-history.json --archive C:/Users/fahad/Downloads/SignatureSolar.7z
```

Review the plan and its `.unresolved.json` companion before applying it. The apply operation requires an explicit production origin and an owner/collector credential supplied through the environment or an ignored local environment file. The endpoint accepts at most 200 rows per batch and rechecks listing identity against the database. Never put credentials in command arguments, repository data or logs. Backfills are performed after schema deployment, outside migrations. Product and preview charts offer **All history**, and archived rows display source attribution and day-only timestamps.
