# Ecosystem brand marks

The catalog uses locally hosted manufacturer website icons or header wordmarks for recognition, with visible brand labels and product counts. Images are decorative so screen readers read each company once. No third-party logo service is required at runtime. Logos remain the property of their respective owners.

Collected 2026-10-05 from the following official pages and observed assets. Raster assets are optimized to at most 96 px; Franklin uses its full header wordmark. Enphase uses the emblem portion of its monochrome header wordmark on an orange badge (SVG viewport only; paths unchanged). SVGs contain no scripts or external references.
